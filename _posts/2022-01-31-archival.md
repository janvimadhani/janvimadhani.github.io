Developed a program that catalogues parallaxes published by the Allegheny Observatory and
matches them with the Yale Parallax Catalogue, SIMBAD, and the written notes of which plates were used to calculate the parallax at the AO.
The goal is to have one searchable database of stars, specific to the AO parallaxes, that gives the full history of its original observation along with
any currently known information.
